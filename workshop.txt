title=Red Days
description=[h1]Red Days[/h1]
description=Your character now has a menstrual cycle. Find and stockpile feminine hygiene products, manage PMS, and don't leave a tampon in too long.
description=
description=Works in singleplayer and multiplayer. Safe to add to an existing save.
description=
description=[h2]The Cycle[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Four phases: menstrual, follicular, ovulation and luteal. Each cycle is randomized (28–34 days by default, with a 2–5 day period).
description=[*]Outside your period, light discharge soils a panty liner once a day.
description=[*]Your health affects your cycle:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Nutrition:[/b] being underweight, overweight or malnourished delays your next period. Severe cases can hold it off for weeks or stop it entirely.
description=[*][b]Trauma:[/b] a serious injury can push your next period back by up to a month.
description=[*][b]Stress:[/b] a long stretch of high stress holds your period back. It takes days of pressure to build up and days of calm to shake off, and it only ever makes you late.
description=[/list]
description=[*]Only female characters by default. Sandbox options can enable it for all genders.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Hygiene Products[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Sanitary pads, tampons and panty liners. Boxes turn up in bathrooms, bedrooms, pharmacies, stores and hospitals, and female zombies sometimes carry a few. You can unpack and repack the boxes.
description=[*]Worn products slowly get used up. Moodles tell you when it's time to change.
description=[*]Go without, or wait too long, and you'll leak: blood stains on your body and clothes, and eventually drips on the ground.
description=[*]Optional sandbox setting: new characters start with a few products.
description=[*]Custom 3D models by [b]sudodski[/b]. Thank you, we appreciate you!
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]PMS[/h2]
description=Each character on spawn has up to 3 Random PMS symptoms that stay the same for life. A sandbox option randomizes them each cycle instead. Symptoms build late in the cycle, peak just before your period, and fade within about a day once it starts. Severity varies from cycle to cycle.
description=Eventually these will be traits
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Agitation[/b]: Uses the game's anger moodle. Faster stamina recovery at peak, which offsets some of Fatigue's drain.
description=[*][b]Fatigue[/b]: Slower stamina recovery and faster tiredness.
description=[*][b]Sadness[/b]: Unhappiness rises and falls with PMS severity.
description=[*][b]Cramps[/b]: Lower torso and groin muscle strain. Can make sleeping hard without painkillers.
description=[*][b]Tender Breasts[/b]: Upper torso muscle strain (lessened if you also have cramps).
description=[*][b]Food Cravings[/b]: You get peckish again soon after eating.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Relief[/b] (the two stack):
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Painkillers[/b]: Reduces symptoms severity for 6 in-game hours.
description=[*][b]Hot water bottle[/b]: fill one with water, heat it on a stove, oven, campfire or in a microwave, then hold it. Up to 30% less PMS while it's hot, fading as it cools over a few hours.
description=[*][b]Comfort food[/b]: Less effective than pain killers and varies by food. Chocolate and chocolate desserts (cake, cookies, doughnuts, candy bars) work best. Milk, yoghurt, cheese, oatmeal, peanuts, peanut butter, bananas, fish, and other sweets (cakes, pies, cookies, candy, ice cream) help too, including when cooked into stews and other meals.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Period Tracker[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Craft a Period Tracker book. Keep it in your inventory or a bag you're carrying, and each time you change a product, the day is logged in its calendar.
description=[*]Each book belongs to one character (the ID on page 14). If nothing is being logged, clear page 14 or craft a new book.
description=[*]A new inspirational quote appears every time you change. Suggestions welcome in the comments!
description=[*]With the start delay enabled, a new character's tracker starts logging after the first period.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS)[/h2]
description=[b]WARNING: TSS IS LETHAL BY DEFAULT.[/b] It can be made non-lethal or turned off in sandbox options.
description=[list]
description=[*]Wearing the same tampon for more than 8 hours can start an infection. A soiled pad or liner is only a risk if you have a groin wound.
description=[*]It starts mild: tiredness, low mood and a TSS moodle, then thirst. Ignored, it can turn into [b]toxic shock[/b]: fever, sickness and health loss. Exhaustion, thirst, hunger and body temperature make it progress faster and hit harder.
description=[*][b]Prevention:[/b] change your products regularly.
description=[*][b]Early stages:[/b] remove the source and your body recovers on its own over time. Antibiotics speed it up.
description=[*][b]Toxic shock:[/b] you need a full course of antibiotics, about 10 doses taken roughly 12 hours apart (about 5 days). Sleeping while the antibiotics work helps you recover health.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]FAQ[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can my period hurt me?[/b] No. Bleeding is shown only through moodles and stains, and never injures you. Only untreated TSS can kill.
description=[*][b]Existing saves?[/b] Safe to add. Products won't appear in places you've already looted, but can still turn up on corpses.
description=[*][b]Do the other phases do anything?[/b] Only the discharge, plus PMS near the end of the cycle.
description=[*][b]Requires:[/b] MoodleFramework.
description=[/list]
